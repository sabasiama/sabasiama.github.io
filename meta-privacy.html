Privacy Policy

Effective: 29 October 2026

This policy covers Kai and Puck Uploader, a private tool operated by Amastrata Media. It publishes videos that Amastrata Media produces to its own Facebook Page, ORBIT Explains, and to the Instagram account linked to that Page. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

Access tokens for Amastrata Media's own Facebook Page and linked Instagram account, the Page and Instagram account IDs and names, and performance statistics (such as views and likes) for posts the app publishes.

How the data is used

Only to publish Amastrata Media's own videos to its own Page and Instagram account and to read statistics for those posts. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

Tokens and statistics are stored only on Amastrata Media's own computer. Data is never sold, shared with, or transferred to third parties.

Data deletion

To remove the app's access, go to Facebook Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations and remove Kai and Puck Uploader. To have all stored data deleted, email biz.amanullah@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion". All stored tokens and statistics are deleted within 30 days.
